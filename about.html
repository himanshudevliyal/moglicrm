About

About Mogli

Mogli is travel management software for agencies, tour operators and DMCs. It brings enquiries, itineraries, bookings and payments into one place.

Get in touch See the features

What Mogli does

One calm workspace for the whole journey, from the first message to the final payment.

Enquiries

Capture every lead from calls, WhatsApp, web forms and email in one inbox, each with a status, an owner and a follow-up reminder.

Itineraries

Build day-wise itineraries and reusable packages, price them in seconds and share a polished proposal your customers can approve.

Bookings

Track hotels, flights and transfers for every trip, with confirmation status your whole team can see at a glance.

Payments

Send payment links, record instalments and see who has paid, who is partial and what is still due, without a spreadsheet.

Your data stays yours

What the privacy policy says, in short.

  • Data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Each organization’s data is separated from every other organization’s and is visible only to that organization’s staff, according to the roles its administrators set.
  • The Android app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries.
  • All traffic between the app or dashboard and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
