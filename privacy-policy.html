How the Mogli CRM Android app and web dashboard handle personal data, and how to request deletion of your account or data.

Delete your account or dataContact

01Who this covers

Mogli CRM is business software used by travel companies (tour operators, destination management companies and travel agents) to manage enquiries, quotations, bookings and payments. The Android app and the dashboard are used by the staff of a company that has an account (“the organization”). The organization decides which of its customers’ details are entered and is responsible for them; Mogli CRM stores and processes that data on the organization’s behalf.

02What is collected

  • Staff accounts: name, email address, phone number, role and sign-in credentials. Passwords are stored only as a hash. If two-factor authentication is switched on, the authenticator secret is stored to verify codes.
  • Business records entered by staff: customer names, contact details, travel dates, travel preferences, quotations, bookings, supplier details, payments and the notes and messages attached to them.
  • Activity records: who changed what and when (an audit log), sign-in times, and technical details of each request such as the IP address, kept in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

The Android app does not access your location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or advertising identifier. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries. Calling, messaging or emailing a customer from the app opens your phone’s own dialler, WhatsApp or mail app; nothing is sent through Mogli CRM.

03How it is used

  • To provide the service: signing you in, showing the organization’s data to the staff allowed to see it, and producing quotations, vouchers, invoices and receipts.
  • To keep the service secure and working: audit trails, access control, abuse prevention and fault diagnosis.
  • To send operational messages the organization has set up, such as payment reminders or booking updates to its customers.

Data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

04Who it is shared with

Data leaves the service only where the organization has chosen to connect a provider, and only what that provider needs: a payment gateway to collect a payment, an email or WhatsApp provider to deliver a message, and the suppliers (hotels, transport, activity providers) a booking is placed with, who receive the traveller details needed for that reservation. Hosting providers that run the servers process data under contract.

05Storage and security

All traffic between the app or dashboard and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS). On the phone, the app keeps only your sign-in tokens, in the device’s secure storage; they are removed when you sign out. Each organization’s data is separated from every other organization’s and is visible only to that organization’s staff, according to the roles its administrators set.

06Delete your Mogli CRM account or data

To delete your account and its data

  1. Email developer@anantavya.com from the email address you sign in to Mogli CRM with, with the subject “Mogli CRM account deletion”.
  2. Include the name of the company (organization) your account belongs to.
  3. We confirm the request with you and with your organization’s administrator, then delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Your organization’s administrator can also deactivate your account straight away from the Mogli CRM dashboard (Settings, Users), which stops all sign-in immediately.

To delete some of your data without deleting your account

Email the same address with the subject “Mogli CRM data deletion” and say which data you want removed (for example, your phone number or profile details). We remove it within 30 days.

If you are a traveller or customer of a travel company

Your details were entered by the travel company you dealt with. Ask that company to correct or delete them; it can do so directly. If it cannot, it can ask us to.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your name, email, phone number, password and two-factor secretDeleted
Your sign-in sessions and notification preferencesDeleted
Your name on the organization’s records (who created a lead, who recorded a payment)Replaced with “Deleted user”; the business records stay with the organization
Invoices, receipts and payment recordsKept by the organization for as long as tax and accounting law requires (up to 8 years in India)
Server logs (IP address, request times)Kept up to 90 days, then deleted automatically
BackupsOverwritten within 30 days

07Retention

Records are kept for as long as the organization’s account is active, because bookings, invoices and payment records have to remain available to it. When an organization closes its account, its data is deleted within 90 days, except records the law requires it to keep.

08Children

The service is for business use by adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and no accounts are created for them. Traveller details of minors appear only where a parent or guardian has booked for them.

09Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions and requests about personal data go to developer@anantavya.com.
