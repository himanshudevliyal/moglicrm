The problem

Why quoting takes so long

Every quote starts from a blank sheet and ends with a lot of copying.

01

Quotes built from scratch

Each itinerary is rebuilt in a spreadsheet, with rates looked up one supplier at a time.

02

Prices out of date

Rates change, but the copy in someone’s file does not, so quotes go out wrong.

03

Margin hidden until later

With cost and price in different places, you only learn the margin after the customer says yes.
