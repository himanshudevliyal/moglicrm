The problem

What happens when follow-ups live in memory

Most lost bookings are not lost to a competitor, they are lost to silence.

01

Follow-ups forgotten

A quote goes out, then nothing. Without a reminder, the lead goes cold before anyone checks back.

02

Suppliers left unconfirmed

03

Everything is written by hand
