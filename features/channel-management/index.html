The problem

What channels look like when managed apart

Selling in many places is good until the places stop agreeing.

01

Each channel a separate job

Rates and rooms are updated channel by channel, so some always lag behind.

02

Rooms sold twice

When a room sells on one channel but not the others, the guest finds out at check-in.

03

No record of what synced

It is hard to say when a channel last received your rates.

How it works

Bookings flow in
